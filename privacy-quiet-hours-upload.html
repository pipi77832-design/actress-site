Privacy Policy — Quiet Hours Upload

Last updated: November 22, 2026

"Quiet Hours Upload" is a personal, single-user tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel "Quiet Hours Sleep". It is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Data the app accesses

The app accesses the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos and to set their titles, descriptions, tags, and visibility. It does not access any other Google service (such as Drive, Gmail, or Photos).

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data of any third party. No user data is transferred to third parties. The only data processed is the owner's own video files and the metadata the owner provides for them.

Tokens and revocation

The OAuth access token used by the app is stored securely and is used only for the uploads described above. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google Account "third-party access" settings page, which immediately stops all uploads by the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact information on the Quiet Hours Sleep YouTube channel page.
